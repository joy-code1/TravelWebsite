About us

A small team of planners, guides and fixers who would rather travel slowly.

Great teamwork

Planners, local guides and drivers who have worked together for years, so a trip runs the way it was designed on the day.

Our vision

Travel that gives more than it takes: small groups, locally owned stays and itineraries paced for the place, not the checklist.

Our mission

To make a properly planned trip something anyone can book — honest prices, no hidden transfers and a real person on the other end.

Our story

We started with one badly planned holiday

Ten days, four cities and a suitcase that spent more time in a coach boot than in a hotel room. We came home convinced there had to be a better way to see a place — fewer stops, better stays, and guides who live where they lead.

So we built it. Every itinerary we sell is one we have walked ourselves, at the pace we would choose for a friend. If a transfer is longer than three hours we tell you before you book, not after.

A good trip is not a longer checklist. It is one place seen properly.

Siti SarahFounder, Travosca
